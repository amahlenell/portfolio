(03 — HOW I BUILD)FROM THE PROBLEM / TO THE DETAILS

Understand it.
Then build it.

My approach to full-stack development
combines clear boundaries, small iterations,
and deliberate use of AI tools.

01

Define the problem.

Start with the person, the task, and what a working solution needs to do. Keep the first version focused.

In practice: Our Table’s recipe-to-meal-plan flow.
02

Design the boundaries.

Separate the interface, domain rules, and service calls. Consider data ownership and authentication before adding features.

In practice: household access policies and server-side validation.
03

Build with intention.

Use Claude and Codex to explore approaches, implement, and debug. Read the code, question assumptions, and refine the result.

AI supports the workflow; it doesn’t replace understanding.
04

Check the edge cases.

Keep calculations testable. Check loading, errors, small screens, and keyboard access. Be clear about what remains unverified.

In practice: timezone rules and unknown nutrition values.
