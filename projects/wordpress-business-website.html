Website development - Illustrative case study

Roofcraft

A conceptual website created for a roof repair and maintenance brand to show how thoughtful design, clear content, and responsive development can build trust and convert interest into enquiries.

01 Overview

Designing a website that reflects the quality of the work.

Roofcraft needed a site that could present its services with confidence, give prospective clients a simple path to enquire, and feel reliable from the very first click. The concept pairs a calm layout with a WordPress content structure the team could maintain without developer support.

RoleStrategy, UX, UI, build
PlatformWordPress
Scope6-page marketing site

02 Challenge

Turning a complex industry into a simple user experience.

  • Clearly communicate multiple construction services.
  • Deliver a seamless experience on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
  • Be easy to update using WordPress.
03 Solution

We shaped the site around the questions a prospective client needs answered: what Axon Comnstruction does, who it is for, why its approach is different, and how to begin. Service pages use structured calls to action; project pages support visual storytelling; an SEO-ready journal creates room for long-term discoverability.
