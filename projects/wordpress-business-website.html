Website development - Illustrative case study

North & Co.
Studio

A refined WordPress website concept for a boutique interiors studio, designed to turn a strong visual identity into a clear, credible online experience.

01 Overview

A digital front door with the same care as the work inside.

North & Co. needed a site that could present its services with confidence, give prospective clients a simple path to enquire, and make its project stories easy to explore. The concept pairs a calm editorial layout with a WordPress content structure the team could maintain without developer support.

RoleStrategy, UX, UI, build
PlatformWordPress
Scope6-page marketing site

02 Challenge

Make quality feel immediate-without making visitors work for it.

  • Distil several specialist services into a clear offer
  • Balance an image-led brand with practical conversion paths
  • Create a flexible, editable system for future projects and articles
03 Solution

We shaped the site around the questions a prospective client needs answered: what North & Co. does, who it is for, why its approach is different, and how to begin. Service pages use structured calls to action; project pages support visual storytelling; an SEO-ready journal creates room for long-term discoverability.
